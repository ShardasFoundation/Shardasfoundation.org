Our Causes

Together for a Cleaner Yamuna

The Yamuna is more than a river—it is a lifeline that deserves our care and respect. Through our river cleaning drives, we bring together volunteers who believe that even small actions can create a cleaner and healthier environment. Every bag of waste removed is a step toward restoring nature for future generations. Our mission is not only to clean the river but also to inspire communities to protect and preserve our natural heritage. Together, we can create a ripple effect of environmental responsibility that lasts for generations.

Together, we can protect the Yamuna and inspire a cleaner tomorrow.

A Rescue Mission with Glowing Ribbons

Every life matters, including the lives of our street animals. We tie reflective glowing ribbons around stray dogs to improve their visibility at night, helping reduce road accidents and making streets safer for both animals and commuters. A simple ribbon can make a life-saving difference. This small initiative has a meaningful impact by protecting innocent animals and promoting compassion within our communities. Together, we can make our streets safer while showing kindness to those who cannot speak for themselves.

Small acts of kindness can protect countless innocent lives.
