VDHM NPO Meet 2026

SHARDA'S HOPE AND HARMONY FOUNDATION was honored to participate in the VDHM NPO Meet, where Dr PRASHANT THAKUR, Managing Director, SHARDA'S HOPE AND HARMONY FOUNDATION, had the privilege of interacting with Mr. Robin Thomas, AGM, HCL, and Mr. Sanjay Bhatia, CSR Head, Genpact. The meeting was highly insightful, and their valuable guidance on CSR partnerships, sustainable development, strategic collaborations, and creating greater social impact was truly inspiring.The discussions focused on strengthening NGO-corporate partnerships, environmental conservation, youth empowerment, and enhancing the implementation of community development initiatives.
